# AI / ML Education, Student & Researcher Programs — Master List

**Compiled 2026-05-19** | Sources: 4 parallel research agents (frontier labs / compute & infra / dev tools / fellowships & grants). All URLs verified via WebSearch on canonical pages where possible; `[unverified]` flag where only third-party listings existed at compile time. Re-verify deadlines and credit amounts before relying on any single row — programs change fast.

## Plain-meta summary

You asked for AI-company offers/programs for students and education — especially the buried, less-obvious ones. Four agents covered disjoint slices: (1) frontier AI labs, (2) compute/infrastructure providers, (3) dev tools and AI-native startups, (4) fellowships, grants, hackathons, and aggregator packs.

**~200 distinct programs surfaced.** The most under-marketed wins worth chasing first:

- **AI Student Pack** (aistudentpack.com) — bundles ~$1,500 worth of credits across Bolt, Lovable, Gamma, Higgsfield, Hedra, HeyGen, Coconote, Sketchy, Granola, ElevenLabs Reader, etc. Volume-limited.
- **Anthropic External Researcher Access Program** — buried in support.claude.com, $500-$25K credits for alignment research.
- **Anthropic AI for Science** — up to $20K Claude API credits for academic researchers.
- **DeepInfra DeepStart** — up to 1B free inference tokens for startups.
- **RunPod Academic Research Credits** — up to $25K GPU credits for labs.
- **Lambda Research Grant** — up to $5K + mentoring from CSO.
- **NVIDIA Academic Hardware Grant** — actual RTX hardware shipped to labs (faculty-gated).
- **Hyperbolic .edu credit** — $15 H100 credit, announced via X, no formal page.
- **OpenAI Codex for Students** — $100 ChatGPT credits, US/Canada, lives on developers.openai.com subdomain.
- **CRA Trustworthy AI Fellowship** — $17K stipend, Microsoft-funded but routed via CRA.
- **DeepMind PhD Scholarships at INSAIT** (Bulgaria) — fully funded.
- **MBZUAI scholarship** (UAE) — AED 15.5-17.5K/mo + tuition + housing.
- **IndiaAI Fellowship** — INR 70-80k/mo, 100 PhD slots/year.
- **Foresight AI for Safety & Science** — ~$3M/yr pool, physical hubs in SF + Berlin.
- **Apple Scholars in AIML** — invite-only, $40K/yr stipend; contact `aiml_scholars@apple.com`.
- **Hugging Face ZeroGPU Community Grants** — RTX Pro 6000 access, applied via discussion threads.
- **OpenAI NextGenAI Consortium** — $50M passed through 15 partner institutions (incl. Harvard, MIT, Oxford, Sciences Po).
- **Snowflake One Million Minds** — $20M upskilling pool, ASEAN/Canada/Germany/India/Korea/LATAM/Saudi focus.
- **Tencent Rhino-Bird Elite** — 3-12 month research visits, ~6% accept rate.

Defunct / closed since last cycle (skip): **Tabnine student plan, Phind (shut Jan 2026), GitHub Copilot Student signups paused 2026-04-20, OpenAI Residency 2026 cycle**.
